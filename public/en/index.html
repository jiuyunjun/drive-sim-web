About This Driving Simulator

A free browser driving simulator game modeled on driving test center and license course routes. No install required — open it in your browser and start practicing sedan or motorcycle driving right away.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this simulator free to play?

Yes, it is completely free and runs directly in your browser. No download or app install is needed.

Can I recreate the actual course from my local test center?

Yes. Upload a top-down map image as the ground texture, and you can practice on a layout close to your local driving test center course.

Does it work on mobile phones?

Yes, the mobile layout supports landscape touch controls — swipe to steer and use on-screen buttons for throttle and brake.

What vehicle types are supported?

A sedan (Corolla) and a motorcycle are currently supported. Switch between them from the vehicle menu to feel the different steering behavior.
